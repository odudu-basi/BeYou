## Description

Every time you mindlessly pick up your phone, BeYou steps in — not to punish you, but to remind you who you're becoming.

BeYou is the only screen time app that combines intelligent app blocking with personalized affirmations, turning every urge to scroll into a moment of growth. Instead of just locking you out, BeYou helps you pause, reflect, and decide intentionally whether you really need that app right now.

SET INTENTIONS, NOT JUST LIMITS
Choose the apps that steal your time and set daily open limits for each one. Want to open Instagram only 5 times a day for 10 minutes each? Done. BeYou tracks every open and gently blocks you when you've hit your goal — but always gives you the choice to continue mindfully.

THE MINDFUL UNLOCK
When you reach for a blocked app, BeYou doesn't just show a lock screen. It shows you affirmations personalized to your goals, your beliefs, and your life. Read them. Speak them. Let them sink in. Then decide — do you still need to open that app?

500+ PERSONALIZED AFFIRMATIONS
Affirmations tailored to who you are — your goals, your faith, your struggles, your dreams. Browse them anytime in the Motivation tab with beautiful nature themes, or encounter them naturally when you try to open a blocked app. Save your favourites and build your own collection.

DISCONNECT SCHEDULES
Set recurring schedules to block distracting apps during the times that matter most. Morning Mindful keeps your first hour phone-free. Night Mode protects your sleep. Deep Focus blocks everything during work. Create as many as you need, each with its own set of blocked apps.

TRACK YOUR GROWTH
Your Discipline Score (0-100) shows how well you're sticking to your intentions. Watch it climb as you build streaks, resist temptation, and choose "Nevermind" over mindless scrolling. See per-app breakdowns, weekly snapshots, and your longest streaks.

DAILY CHALLENGES
Build healthy habits alongside your screen time goals. Check off daily challenges like reading, drinking water, exercising, or journaling. Small wins that compound into a better life.

BEAUTIFUL & PERSONAL
Choose from 17 stunning nature themes, 9 solid colors, and 3 gradients for your affirmation experience. Pick from 10 unique app icons to make BeYou yours. Every detail is designed to feel calm, intentional, and personal.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
BeYou works entirely on your device using Apple's Screen Time API. Your usage data, affirmations, and personal information never leave your phone. No accounts. No cloud tracking. Just you and your growth.
